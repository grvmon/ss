The Student Rent Drain: Why Paying Summer Break PG Rent Makes No Sense

Every May and June, hundreds of thousands of university students across Delhi University (North and South Campus), IP University, Jamia Millia Islamia, and Amity University face the same frustrating financial dilemma. With colleges closing for the 8-to-12-week summer vacation, students return home to their home states. Yet, paying guest (PG) owners and private flat landlords demand full rent just to let empty bags, study notes, and furniture sit in a locked room.

Paying ₹15,000 to ₹25,000 each month for an empty room amounts to ₹40,000 to ₹75,000 in dead, unrecoverable capital. Modern student self-storage solutions provide an ingenious workaround: vacate your PG, move your belongings into an affordable secure locker, and return in August with your savings intact.

6 Smart Ways College Students Save Space & Money

1. Eliminate 2 to 3 Months of Summer Break Rent

By relinquishing your PG tenancy before summer break and checking into student storage, you instantly eliminate your largest recurring student expense. When the autumn semester resumes in August, you have complete freedom to hunt for fresh PG options or upgraded flat shares near campus without being trapped in an unfavorable contract.

2. Pool Storage Units with Friends to Pay Pocket-Change Tariffs

You don\'t need to rent an entire storage suite alone. Classmates and roommates regularly co-rent a single 35 to 50 sq ft private room. Stacking 4–5 boxes each, along with rolled mattresses and study chairs, splits the already economical monthly rate among 3 to 4 friends, bringing individual costs down to just a few hundred rupees per month.

3. Pay-Per-Box Options for Light Travel

For students living in furnished college hostels who only own study books, winter blankets, kitchen utensils, and suitcases, renting a physical room is unnecessary. Pay-per-box storage charges a minimal per-carton rate. You pack standard boxes, hand them over, and retrieve them when college reopens.

4. Keep Laptops, Gaming Consoles & Valuables 100% Secure

Leaving valuable laptops, gaming PCs, cameras, musical instruments, and high-end monitors unattended in shared hostel rooms or unlocked summer PGs carries high theft and pest damage risks. Purpose-built facilities offer 24/7 CCTV surveillance, fire prevention systems, and private key locks that guarantee total protection while you relax at home.

5. Preserve Competitive Exam Materials (UPSC, CAT, GATE)

Heavy binders of handwritten coaching notes, standard reference tomes, and past-year test papers represent years of academic labor. Leaving them in damp hostel corners risks termite infestation and monsoon water damage. Storage units maintain a clean, dry, climate-shielded environment that ensures your reference material remains pristine. Check out our guide on organizing study tables and materials for study room setups.

6. Doorstep Pickup Directly from Your Campus Hostel

College students without personal vehicles don\'t need to struggle hauling heavy boxes across town. Professional logistics partners coordinate doorstep pickups straight from North Campus, South Campus, Greater Noida, or Gurugram hostels, transporting your inventory directly to the secure facility.
