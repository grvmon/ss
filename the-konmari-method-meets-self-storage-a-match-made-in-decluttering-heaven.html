The KonMari Method Meets Self Storage: A Match Made in Decluttering Heaven

Introduction: 

Marie Kondo’s KonMari method has taken the world by storm, teaching people how to declutter their homes and lives by focusing on items that “spark joy.” But what do you do with the items that don’t quite fit the joy-sparking criteria, yet still hold value? The answer is simple: self storage. In this blog, we’ll explore how combining the KonMari method with self storage can create a harmonious, clutter-free living space. Combining the KonMari method with self-storage is a match made in decluttering heaven, bringing organization and spaciousness to every corner of your home. 

1. Embrace the KonMari Philosophy: 

The first step to decluttering using the KonMari method is to change your mindset. Instead of focusing on what to discard, concentrate on the items that bring joy and meaning to your life. This approach will help you make mindful decisions about what to keep, discard, or store. 

2. Sort Items by Category, Not by Location: 

The KonMari method encourages sorting belongings by category, rather than by room or location. This approach allows you to see the full scope of your possessions, making it easier to assess their value and decide whether to keep, discard, or store them. 

3. Determine What to Keep, Discard, or Store: 

As you go through each category, ask yourself if each item sparks joy. If it does, keep it. If not, decide whether to discard or store it. Items with sentimental value, seasonal use, or potential future use are prime candidates for self storage.

By utilizing the decluttering heaven technique of determining what to keep, discard, or store, you can effortlessly create a harmonious living space that reflects your truest self.  

4. Choose the Right Self Storage Facility: 

Finding a suitable self storage facility is essential for a seamless KonMari experience. Look for facilities that offer climate-controlled units, secure access, and flexible hours to ensure your belongings are well-protected and accessible when needed. 

5. Organize Your Self Storage Unit: 

Organize your storage unit in a way that aligns with the KonMari method. Use clear storage bins, label boxes, and group items by category. This will make it easier to find and retrieve items when needed, and ensure your unit remains clutter-free and easy to navigate. 

6. Practice Gratitude for Your Stored Items: 

In the spirit of the KonMari method, practice gratitude for the items you choose to store. Acknowledge the value they’ve brought to your life, and remember that storing them allows you to maintain a clutter-free living space without sacrificing their presence entirely. 

7. Maintain Your Clutter-Free Home: 

Once you’ve decluttered using the KonMari method and stored items in self storage, make an effort to maintain your clutter-free home. Regularly assess your belongings and practice mindfulness when acquiring new items, ensuring they align with your joy-sparking criteria. 

Conclusion: 

The KonMari method and self storage are a match made in decluttering heaven. By combining these two powerful tools, you can create a living space that’s not only free of clutter but also filled with items that truly bring joy to your life. Say goodbye to clutter and hello to a more organized, harmonious home.
