Things to Keep in Mind during Bike Storage

For safety measures, no inflammable items are allowed within the storage units of Self Storage India. So, we advise you to remove excess fuel before depositing the bike. You should empty the tank and allow everything to evaporate. If you intend to visit the unit frequently, you can add a fuel stabilizer and ride the bike to improve circulation.

Here are some other precautionary measures:

  • Pull out the battery so that it doesn’t drain and die.
  • Overinflate the tires to prevent soft spots or prop it on the center stand
  • Spread a tarp or bedsheet to protect from dirt and dust
  • Cover up the exhaust pipe to prevent insects or rodents from entering
