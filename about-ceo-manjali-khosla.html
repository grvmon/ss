The Inception: Born Out of a Personal Challenge

Manjali Khosla’s journey to founding Self Storage India began with a very real, relatable dilemma. While relocating from Dubai to India, she needed a storage facility to temporarily house her household furniture and personal belongings. She searched across Delhi NCR but struggled to find a single facility that was clean, easily accessible, pest-controlled, and woman-friendly.

Having lived and worked abroad for years, she was surprised by the absence of organized self-storage in India — especially given that private lockable storage has long been a staple service in developed metropolitan economies worldwide.

Rather than settling for unorganized warehousing or damp basements, her entrepreneurial spirit kicked in. In 2013, she founded Self Storage India — pioneering the nation's first purpose-built, secure, private self-storage facility born out of her own quest for reliable, dignified space.

school

The Woman Behind the Company

Manjali grew up in Hong Kong in a traditional family where women were customarily expected to complete high school and transition immediately to domestic responsibilities. She chose an alternative, pioneering path — becoming the first woman in her family to pursue higher academic studies in science.

She traveled to the United States and earned a PhD in Analytical Chemistry from the prestigious University of California, Davis (UC Davis). Her scientific background instilled a meticulous, data-driven approach to operations, quality control, humidity mitigation, and systematic security.

Carrying a proud history of "firsts," Dr. Khosla aspires to be the first in her family to steer an enterprise to a public stock exchange listing, and she is steadfastly guiding Self Storage India toward sustainable, institutional growth.

“The idea is, wherever one may be in the world, they know that their goods are safe and sound here – that’s my mission since I know this is what I would want from a company like Self Storage.”

Conscious Living & Social Responsibility

A strong believer in the philosophy, “If you are going to do something, give it your best shot,” Manjali balances executive leadership with deep social and environmental activism.

As co-founder of Care for Air, an active non-profit civic initiative dedicated to combating air pollution, she advocates tirelessly for environmental awareness, respiratory wellness, and clean energy in urban India.

Her dedication to community extends into grassroots education. In her personal time, Dr. Khosla volunteers teaching English to underprivileged youth and coordinates toy and educational game drives to bring joyful, constructive play into children’s lives.

The Vision: Space for Modern, Nomadic Lifestyles

As modern work culture transforms, Self Storage India plays an essential role in unlocking freedom for individuals and businesses alike. The rapid rise of hybrid work, global mobility, and digital nomadism means professionals no longer need to be chained to expensive apartment leases just to safeguard their belongings.

Having a reliable, private storage unit enables individuals to relocate freely between cities, travel abroad without rent burdens, or move back home on their own terms.

For commercial enterprises, flexible month-to-month warehousing eliminates burdensome multi-year real estate leases, allowing startups and SMEs to scale inventory dynamically with zero capital lock-in.
