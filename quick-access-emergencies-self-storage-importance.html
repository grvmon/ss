The Historical Evolution of Self Storage Units

The concept of storing belongings outside of one’s home isn’t new. Ancient civilizations, from the Chinese to the Romans, had methods of external storage. Over time, as urbanization increased and living spaces shrank, the modern self-storage industry was born. Today, it’s a multi-billion dollar industry, with facilities ranging from basic sheds to climate-controlled units.
