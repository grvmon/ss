1. Introduction, Scope & User Consent

At Self Storage India (operated by Private Storage Solutions Pvt. Ltd., accessible from https://selfstorageindia.com), one of our main priorities is the privacy, security, and confidentiality of our visitors, customers, and corporate partners. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information collected, recorded, and processed by Self Storage India and how we utilize it.

This Privacy Policy applies to our online activities and is valid for visitors to our website with regard to the information that they shared and/or collected in Self Storage India. This policy is also applicable to operational personal data provided during space rental onboarding, facility entry, and customer support interactions across our Delhi NCR facilities (Gurugram, Delhi Dwarka, Noida).

check_circle Formal Consent Statement

By using our website, requesting a storage quote, submitting an inquiry, or executing a lease agreement with Self Storage India, you hereby consent to our Privacy Policy and agree to its terms and conditions.

2. Personal Information We Collect

The personal information that you are asked to provide, and the explicit reasons why you are asked to provide it, will be made clear to you at the point we ask you to provide your information:

Categories of Data Collected

  • Direct Inquiries & Communications: If you contact us directly via phone, email, contact forms, or live chat, we may receive additional information about you such as your name, email address, phone number, city, the contents of your message, attached inventory files, and any other information you choose to provide.
  • Account & Lease Registration Info: When you register for a storage account or sign a rental lease, we ask for contact and identity details including your full legal name, company name, residential address, business address, email address, mobile number, and alternate emergency contacts.
  • KYC & Legal Verification Documents: For lease agreement compliance and security gate authorization, we collect government-issued identification (Aadhaar Card, Passport, PAN Card, Voter ID) and corporate incorporation documents (GSTIN certificate, Certificate of Incorporation).
  • Billing & Payment Records: Transaction details, bank transfer records, billing addresses, and payment confirmation IDs. Note: Full credit card numbers or UPI PINs are processed via PCI-DSS compliant banking gateways and are never stored on our servers.

3. How We Use Your Information

Self Storage India uses the collected information in various lawful ways to deliver high-quality storage services, including to:

Purpose of Data Processing Operational Activity & Description
Service Provision & Booking Provide, operate, manage, and maintain our self-storage rooms, lockers, box storage, and warehouse facility operations.
Customer Communication & Support Communicate with you directly or through customer support advisors for billing, service updates, rental reminders, and quote requests.
Identity Verification & Security Verify customer identity (KYC), authorize facility entrance cards, manage nominated representatives, and prevent unauthorized unit access.
Website Optimization & Analytics Analyze site traffic trends, understand user navigation patterns, and improve digital storage calculators and room selection tools.
Fraud Prevention & Legal Compliance Detect and prevent fraudulent activities, financial irregularities, security breaches, and fulfill statutory accounting and police audit requirements.

4. Log Files, IP Addresses & Technical Analytics

Self Storage India follows a standard procedure of using web server log files. These files log visitors when they visit websites. All professional web hosting services perform this as part of infrastructure management and hosting analytics.

The information collected by log files includes Internet Protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date and time stamp, referring/exit pages, and the number of clicks. These details are not linked to any information that is personally identifiable. The purpose of this information is strictly for analyzing web performance, administering the site, tracking user movement on the website, and gathering broad demographic insight.

5. Cookies, Web Beacons & Tracking Technologies

Like almost all modern websites, Self Storage India uses 'cookies'. Cookies are small text files stored on your browser to record user preferences, optimize session performance, and customize webpage content based on visitor browser type and browsing history.

Types of Cookies Used

  • Essential Functional Cookies: Required for site navigation, quote calculator calculations, modal form submissions, and secure session management.
  • Performance & Analytics Cookies: Web analytics tools (such as Google Analytics / GTM) collect aggregated, anonymous data to measure visitor engagement and site performance.
  • Advertising & Remarketing Cookies: Third-party ad networks (such as Google Ads, Meta Pixel) may place cookies to measure ad campaign effectiveness and serve relevant storage solutions when you visit other websites.

You can choose to disable or selectively turn off cookies through your individual browser settings (Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge). Detailed information about cookie management with specific web browsers can be found on their respective official websites.

6. Third-Party Privacy Policies & Ad Networks

Self Storage India’s Privacy Policy does not apply to external websites, third-party advertisers, or linked platforms. Thus, we strongly advise you to consult the respective Privacy Policies of these third-party services for detailed information regarding their data practices and opt-out instructions.

Third-party ad servers or ad networks use technologies like cookies, JavaScript, or Web Beacons in their respective advertisements that appear on Self Storage India, which are sent directly to users' browsers. They automatically receive your IP address when this occurs. Self Storage India has no access to or control over cookies used by third-party advertisers.

7. Physical Facility Security & CCTV Data Policy

In addition to digital privacy, Self Storage India maintains strict physical data and security management rules across all storage facilities in Gurugram, Delhi Dwarka, and Noida:

  • 24/7 CCTV Recording: Common hallways, loading bays, entry gates, and building perimeters are continuously recorded by high-definition CCTV security cameras for safety and loss prevention.
  • Video Footage Confidentiality: CCTV footage is stored securely on encrypted physical servers. Video logs are never published or shared with third parties, except when requested by statutory law enforcement agencies, police audits, or formal court orders.
  • Visitor & Vehicle Access Logs: Gate logbooks and digital visitor management systems capture visitor names, contact numbers, vehicle registration numbers, and entry/exit timestamps to safeguard facility integrity.

8. California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) Rights

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), among other rights, California consumers have the right to request that a business that collects a consumer's personal data disclose the categories and specific pieces of personal data collected, request deletion of personal data, and request that personal data not be sold.

do_not_distribute CCPA Notice: We Do Not Sell Personal Data

Self Storage India does not sell personal data to third parties. If you submit a CCPA data request, we have one month to respond to you. If you would like to exercise any of these rights, please contact us.

9. General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) Rights

We would like to make sure you are fully aware of all of your data protection rights. Every user is entitled to the following rights under GDPR standards:

  • The Right to Access: You have the right to request copies of your personal data. We may charge a nominal administrative fee for this service if requests are repetitive.
  • The Right to Rectification: You have the right to request that we correct any information you believe is inaccurate or complete information you believe is incomplete.
  • The Right to Erasure: You have the right to request that we erase your personal data under certain conditions, provided statutory tax and legal lease retention periods have elapsed.
  • The Right to Restrict Processing: You have the right to request that we restrict the processing of your personal data under certain conditions.
  • The Right to Object to Processing: You have the right to object to our processing of your personal data under certain conditions.
  • The Right to Data Portability: You have the right to request that we transfer the data that we have collected to another organization, or directly to you, under certain conditions.

If you make a formal request, we have one (1) month to respond to you. If you would like to exercise any of these rights, please contact our Data Protection Officer.

10. Children's Online Privacy Protection (COPPA)

Another key priority is adding protection for children while using the internet. We encourage parents and guardians to observe, participate in, and/or monitor and guide their children's online activity.

Self Storage India does not knowingly collect any Personally Identifiable Information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that your child has provided this kind of information on our website, we strongly encourage you to contact us immediately, and we will make our best efforts to promptly remove such information from our records.

11. Data Security, Encryption & Retention Protocols

To safeguard your personal information against accidental loss, unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure, Self Storage India enforces administrative, technical, and physical security measures:

  • TLS/SSL Encryption: All data transmissions over our website are encrypted using standard Transport Layer Security (TLS/SSL) technology.
  • Restricted Staff Access: Access to customer KYC records, contract documents, and account databases is strictly restricted to authorized staff members on a need-to-know basis.
  • Data Retention Period: We retain personal data for as long as your storage lease account remains active, and subsequently for the statutory retention periods prescribed under Indian tax, corporate, and contract law.

12. Updates to This Privacy Policy

Self Storage India reserves the right to update or modify this Privacy Policy at any time to reflect operational changes, legal updates, or service enhancements. When changes are made, the revised policy will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date at the top of the page. We encourage visitors to review this policy periodically.
