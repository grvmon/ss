How to Design Your Home Office

Manjali Khosla Co-Founder & Director • Published Oct 26, 2022 • 4 min read • verified Fact-Checked

Are you thinking about putting in a home office? Whether your home office is part of your home, a small bedroom, or just a corner of a room, there are a few essentials to consider when designing a productive workspace at home. If you spend the entire work week or just a few hours there, it is critical that your home office is a welcoming and comfortable place to spend personal time and get your work done efficiently. 

Setting up an Inspiring Home Office 

If you follow a hybrid work model, you and your remote employees will need to create areas for concentration or private offices at home. We have gathered some inspiring suggestions for creating a home office design that enhances focus and productivity. Self Storage India can help you get organised and supply ample space in your home, for when you want to swap out office equipment, accent pieces and more. 

Consider setting up a dedicated home office from a place where there are no interruptions. In fact, according to a few studies, having a dedicated workstation can increase output. 

 Here are a few suggestions for making your home office the best fit for you: 

1. Chose a Scent that best suits you 

Scents that go with your work mood should not be overlooked. While working late, you could want a relaxing scent, while at lunch you might want something energising. You can also pick a scent that goes well with the surroundings of your workplace.  

Whether it is essential oils or coffee beans, some offices have a distinct odour. Pick a smell that complements your unique preferences and way of life. Consider something light and breezy if, for instance, you prefer to work in jeans and a T-shirt. Choose something warm and comfortable if you prefer sweaters and pants.  

2. Decide where you will sit 

Selecting a comfortable place to sit is a key part in designing a stately home office. Find a chair that supports your lower back if you plan to spend a lot of time sitting down. You do not want to slouch for hours in front of a computer. Also, arrange your worktable at a comfortable height so you avoid eye strain.   

Why not invest in a table that can be adjusted to the eye level making it easier on your eyes, especially if you are working online for the most part. Thoroughly make sure that there are enough sockets and plug points to ensure uninterrupted workflow. 

3. Confirm that there is ample space around you 

Make sure you allow enough space to work comfortably. Consider whether you will be able to easily move from side to side, stand up, and sit back at your desk. While this may seem obvious, it is easy to underestimate how much space you need.  

Whether you want to stretch your legs or move around to ensure productivity, having ample amount of space is ideal. This can entail including added bookcases, filing cabinets, or a standing desk. 

4. Order your supplies 

When working on a project, you do not want to lose time looking for files or documents. Keep things nearby instead. To store critical items like receipts, instruction booklets, and warranties, use clear containers and boxes. Make sure that your tabletop surfaces are clear to avoid crowding and clutter. 

5. Storage and Shelving 

Storage is often overlooked when designing a home office, but it can have a significant impact on productivity. Include enough shelving, filing cabinets, or cupboards to meet your storage needs. Consider your workflow: what comes in, where it stays while being worked on, and where it goes when finished. 

If you have a lot of loose papers, you can keep a box nearby and to keep easy access to your current work. Do not forget about reference materials – if you use manuals, catalogues, or reference books, keep them near your desk on a shelf. Once you are done with the papers you can recycle them or even invest in a shredder. 

If you’re lucky enough to have some free space in your house, you deserve more than a metal table and an extra chair crammed into a corner. It could be used as a home office, a remote workspace, or even just a place to pay bills and organise your schedule.. An office that reflects the style and comfort of the rest of your home is a place where you know you can count on to stay up late to do your best.
