Declutter your kitchen is essential for creating an organized and efficient cooking space. A clutter-free kitchen looks appealing, enhances functionality, and reduces stress. Begin by assessing your current kitchen layout and identifying problem areas. Start with the basics: clear countertops and cabinets of unnecessary items. Storage solutions such as drawer organizers, shelf dividers, and clear bins are used to keep everything in its place. Group similar items together and label containers to make finding what you need easy.

Consider implementing vertical storage and utilizing cabinet doors for additional space. Regularly purge expired or unused items to prevent overcrowding. This process will help you maximize available space and streamline your kitchen workflow. Additionally, adopting a routine for maintaining organization will ensure your kitchen remains tidy and functional.

Why to Declutter Your Kitchen?

Decluttering your kitchen is crucial for several reasons. First, it enhances functionality by creating a more organized space, making it easier to find and access the items you need while cooking. A tidy kitchen reduces the time spent searching for utensils or ingredients, streamlining your cooking process.

Second, decluttering promotes safety. A clutter-free kitchen minimizes the risk of accidents, such as spills or falls, and reduces the likelihood of kitchen fires by keeping flammable materials away from heat sources.

Third, it contributes to a more pleasant and stress-free cooking environment. An organized kitchen is more inviting and can make meal preparation feel less like a chore.

Finally, regular decluttering helps maintain your kitchen’s cleanliness. By reducing clutter, you make cleaning surfaces easier and preventing the buildup of dust and grime, ensuring a healthier cooking space.

Here are six unbreakable guidelines for organising your kitchen:

1. Keep it Basic

Keeping things simple is the most crucial guideline for decluttering a kitchen. Avoid carrying out too many tasks at once if you want to keep your kitchen clean. Instead, pay attention to one thing at a time. Start by organising your refrigerator, for instance. The pantry and freezer should follow. Next, take on the cabinets. And lastly, clean the countertops. Go on to the later challenge once you have completed each section. This way, you will not get overwhelmed by everything you have to do.

You can use a vacuum cleaner or mop to remove dust or dirt. Then, wipe down all surfaces using a damp cloth. Make sure to also remove any food spills from the floor.

2. Do not Have Endless Spares

Give unused stuff to a worthy cause. Another excellent method to declutter your kitchen is to donate unwanted stuff. Donating something enables someone else to receive help from it, and it also frees up kitchen space in your home. 

Utilise what you currently have instead of buying extra disposable plates, glasses, and cups, which you must always have on hand. Go for reusable items. Avoid having too many spare plastic containers. Fewer plates and cups mean less clutter on kitchen surfaces and cabinets.

3. Be Practical with where you Place Things

To begin with, consider not just where you store your often-used kitchen items but also how you arrange the less often-used ones. This includes large objects like pots and pans and infrequently used objects like baking dishes. Keep spices and condiments handy and close to the cooking area. If an object is heavy, place it somewhere low down; otherwise, it could topple over and injure you. Similarly, store rarely used items—such as special occasion cutlery or your handmade pasta maker—in a box with a clear label so you can easily retrieve them when needed.

If you have a cluttered kitchen, you might want to consider how much room each item occupies and whether it is needed. Avoid squandering priceless cabinet space. Furthermore, you do not want to spend hours searching through drawers for something when you need it regularly.

4. Rotate your kitchen, seasonally

A very effective decluttering approach is to rotate your entire kitchen seasonally. Depending on what you use when, you can store it in a closet or a storage area. Move it in and out as needed. Why not use the same reasoning you use for your kitchen when you have a summer and a winter closet?

Here is how it works: Until the festival season, store your holiday decorations and other seasonal items in designated plastic bins in another storage area of your home. During the colder months, you will only use items like roasting pans, your slow cooker, bake wear, your Kaju barfi or ladoo-making gear, and festive dishes.

Clean out your summer cookware before bringing your fall and winter appliances back to the kitchen (like your ice cream maker, beverage dispenser, summer tablecloths, and all your grilling gear). Are you concerned about where to store these things? The secret is to keep everything you need for the season artfully arranged in labelled bins in your self-storage unit. This method will keep you on top of the clutter accumulating in your kitchen.

5. Clean off your Counters

When you clean off your counters, you eliminate all the minor things that are no longer needed, such as expired receipts, takeaway menu flyers, or unopened mail. Most people have certain items on their kitchen counter that they do not use often but should still have around, just in case. So, what do you do with these things that take up room and sit there?

Hooks are excellent for organising such clutter and creating more space. They are offered in both wooden and plastic varieties and come in various sizes and forms. They are simple to set up, and you can discover them in every hardware store. Use fridge or counter magnets to put up reminders artfully. For example, keep baskets or beautiful trays to store unopened mail or receipts.

6. Decorate simply

Everyone in the house enjoys visiting the kitchen, one of those rooms with the most energy. However, a lot of individuals disregard how detrimental chaotic kitchens can be. Here are some fundamental suggestions to keep in mind if you want to decorate your kitchen:

a. Keep it elegant

Although you might feel that the kitchen needs a lot of colour and ornamentation, keeping things straightforward is quite acceptable. There are several ways to enhance the room’s visual appeal without making it appear congested. Avoid using a variety of finishes and materials throughout the area and instead use a few colours and textures, like real wood and stone.

b. Avoid going too far

Also, be careful not to overdo the decorations. Even though you will want to incorporate some features into the design, do not use them excessively. As a result, you will not feel overburdened when cooking or cleaning.

c. Be innovative

You might want to incorporate a bar counter near the stovetop if you want to simplify cooking. In addition to offering more storage, this prevents spills from spreading widely. Installing a pull-out drawer under the sink for supplies like dish soap and hand sanitizer is another choice to keep these items out of sight. Thanks to these simple adjustments, you will spend less time looking for things while cooking meals.


Being practical and realistic will help you organise your kitchen, according to Vicky Silverthorn, a professional decluttering expert whose goal is to assist individuals in creating more peaceful and organised living environments. Most of us tend to remain with how we piled things in when we first moved into the house, but you can add up to 50% additional storage space in a cupboard by just utilising the arrangement most effectively.

Final Words – Declutter Your Kitchen

By organizing your kitchen effectively, you streamline meal preparation, reduce stress, and enhance safety by minimizing hazards. A clutter-free environment makes it easier to find and use your kitchen tools and helps maintain cleanliness, making regular cleaning more manageable. Embracing decluttering practices ensures that your kitchen remains a pleasant and efficient space for all your culinary needs. Implementing these strategies improves your cooking experience and contributes to a more organized and harmonious home. Regular upkeep and thoughtful organization are vital to keeping your kitchen in shape.
