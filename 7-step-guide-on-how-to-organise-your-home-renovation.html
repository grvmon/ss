1. Start With Design and Planning 

Planning is the best approach to guarantee success on any renovation project. When you start a project with a well-thought-out plan in place, you will save money. It is always important to keep in mind that you should set aside at least 10% of your budget for unexpected costs. Every part of the project, including the budget, materials, timetables, etc., should be considered during planning and design. Why not hire an experienced contractor who can help you plan and design your ideal makeover?

Renovating your home can be overwhelming, but with our guide on how to organise your home renovation, you’ll be prepared for every step of the process. 

Look at these factors to think about before renovating a home: 

  1. What kind of renovation are you looking for? 
  1. Do you need brand-new rooms or merely a facelift? 
  1. What amount of storage space do you have? Is it enough to store not just clothes and small items but also furniture/ books/ appliances? 
  1. Do you prefer a more traditional or modern appearance? 
  1. Can you afford to renovate your house entirely? 

Knowing exactly what you want from your renovation project will help you make the right decisions and even save money. 

2. Organising and Prep 

It is always best to prepare and organise your space before starting any work. You will not need to waste time looking for what you need, instead everything is in its place neat and organised. Here are seven things to look at beforehand: 

  1. First it is ideal to arrange and organize your tools, materials, and supplies. 
  1. Second, give your home a thorough dusting, get rid of any cobwebs and hidden treasures. 
  1. Use old bedsheet as coverups for furniture and delicate pieces to avoid unnecessary paints and spillage. 
  1. Next, make a list of items to buy, rent etc. 
  1. It is always good to have back up options even if you are 100 % sure that you will like certain paints or lights for a specific room, you may end up not liking it once it is installed. 
  1. Store critical papers and documents in a ready to access and safe place. 
  1. Finally, make sure you and your family known the ins and outs of renovation and everyone is on the same page. It can be a stressful time for everyone, and minute details can really help everyone in the process. 

 

Before you start your home renovation project, take the time to organise and prep. Our guide on how to organise your home renovation can help.

4. Make a Master List 

Make a comprehensive list of everything you must carry out before the day begins. To keep track of what you already have against what you still need to buy, use a spreadsheet or online tool. Creating a list and naming what you have, makes sure that you do not overlook the intricate details, such as roller coverings, enough tubs of paint, paintbrushes, etc. Remember it is completely fine to make changes as you go; you are your main priority, if you like it then have no qualms about it. Even if you cannot get everything at once, starting early will help you save time and money in the long run.

5. Do not Forget the Pets 

It’s simple to forget about the pets living inside the house when remodelling among all the hustle and bustle. Don’t let them go unnoticed; it can be a confusing time for pets too. Here are four suggestions to make sure your pets are at ease when work is being done: 

  1. Install pet doors and safety gates for the stairwell. 
  1. Keep electrical cords away from furniture and walls. 
  1. Consider buying a dog box as temporary housing if there is no room for one. Even a laundry basket or shoe box might be repurposed as a temporary kennel. 
  1. If all this is too much hassle, you can even have someone pet-sit for you during the renovation time. 

6. Check the Weather 

India Meteorological Department (IMD) shares daily weather forecasts and warnings, including those for rain and storms. It is always good to check the forecast before beginning any outdoor project, including landscaping, painting, roofing, construction, or anything else. Avoid working outside if rain or a storm is predicted unless you are prepared with the necessary tools and supplies. You can end up damaging costly equipment or supplies, or even the cost for crew and contractors can be saved if the weather causes no work to be done. Regardless of the forecast, it is always the best idea to have a fallback strategy. 

7. Book the Help 

It can be challenging to decide whether you need professional contractor’s services. Making sure you have a signed contract before beginning any renovations with a contractor is a key part of getting ready for a renovation. Don’t forget to inform the contractors with crucial details like the address, the beginning date, and the expected completion date so you and your family can be aware of the coordination. To ensure you receive the finest help from your contractor, keep the following four aspects in mind when you decide you need their help: 

  1. Make it clear what you want done and how much it will cost. 
  1. If anything does not seem quite right, do not be hesitant to ask for clarification. 
  1. If there is something that you know do not want, you must speak up. 
  1. Work with a contractor who will adhere to your schedule and timeline. 

A house renovation project can be approached in a variety of ways. Some property owners prefer to work on their renovations room by room, beginning in the kitchen and moving on to the living room. Others choose to take a more comprehensive approach and assess the entire house before starting any renovations. Whichever approach you decide with, these pointers will serve you well and answer any more remaining questions.
