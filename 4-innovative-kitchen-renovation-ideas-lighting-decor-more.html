Kitchen Renovation Ideas in India 

From vibrant color palettes inspired by Indian culture to incorporating space-saving modular designs, the choices are as diverse as the country itself. Consider adding efficient storage solutions to accommodate the rich array of spices and utensils that define Indian cuisine. Embrace natural light with large windows, allowing the warm Indian sun to grace your culinary space.

Think about incorporating eco-friendly materials and energy-efficient appliances, aligning with sustainable practices. Whether it’s infusing traditional elements or adopting contemporary styles, the options for your kitchen renovation in India is vast and exciting. It is promising a space that not only caters to your cooking needs but also reflects the Indian heritage.

Kitchen Renovation Idea in India – Lighting 

Even though the kitchen is considered the heart of the Indian home, the living room receives all the natural lighting and fancy chandeliers. When choosing lights, most people do not consider the kitchen. Making provisions for adequate natural light during the day and sufficient illumination at dusk, on the other hand, is an important aspect of your kitchen’s ambience. 

The kitchen is the hub of your everyday cooking and often, dining as well. Natural light aids in cleansing the kitchen of microorganisms that might otherwise thrive in dark, dank corners. If your current design is lacking in natural lighting, consider installing a window or a skylight to let in more light. Choose designs for artificial lights that can be easily cleaned of smoke and oil deposits.

Are you tired of the same old kitchen? Spice things up with our kitchen renovation ideas, covering everything from lighting to decor. 

Kitchen Renovation Idea in India – Colours 

Consider the colour tones and scheme of the rest of the house when creating a palette for your kitchen too. Soothing colours contrasted with small splashes of bright colours can make your kitchen stand out without being garish. Most urban homes these days have jewel tones, but subdued colours like beige or light shades of blue, yellow, or green also work well. It is best to match the palettes of both rooms if you have an open kitchen, especially one that opens into the living room. When deciding on a kitchen colour scheme, you must consider more than just the colour of the walls. The colour of your walls must match the colour of your cabinets and any other furniture in the room. If you can get your appliances to match, that would be ideal. 

  • Select colours that complement one another and work well together. 
  • Avoid using too many patterns or bright colours. 
  • Consider how the colours will appear against several types of surfaces. Dark wood floors, for example, will absorb some of the lighter tones, while a white kitchen will reflect most of the colours. 

 

Budget tip: If painting all the walls in the kitchen is out of the question, simply adding a unique, new backsplash will make all the difference.

 

From modernizing your lighting fixtures to adding colorful accents, our kitchen renovation ideas are sure to spark your creativity and help you achieve a kitchen that’s both functional and stylish. 

 

Add a Touch of Green in Your Kitchen

For a fresh look, add a few small potted plants to the kitchen decor. If your kitchen has a balcony, you can turn it into a small herb garden and grow your own spices, herbs, and microgreens. If you do not have much space, you could put small pots on the windowsill or install wall-mounted shelves. There are many indoor plants that are effective air purifiers to choose from.

Upgrade Appliances 

Renovation is an excellent time to consider upgrading your kitchen appliances. Get rid of the obsolete ones and store the seasonal ones. Invest in modern appliances that are more functional and will complement your upscaled kitchen. 

Modern kitchen appliances can provide: 

  • Increased efficiency 
  • Greater comfort 
  • Improved operation 
  • Reduced energy consumption 
  • Intelligent technology 

You could also consider buying used small appliances if you are going to replace them. There are many places to find high-quality used kitchen appliances that look brand new. Used appliances are significantly less expensive than new ones. Purchasing small appliances such as a rice cooker or blender adds personality to your kitchen. Look for appliances that will blend in with your existing appliances, making them an inexpensive way to update the look of your kitchen.
