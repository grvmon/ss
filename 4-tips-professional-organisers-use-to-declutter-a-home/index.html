Professional Home Organisers Guide – An Overview

This guide is essential for anyone looking to create a stress-free and organized home. Decluttering is vital for improving both mental and physical spaces. By following expert tips, you can transform your home into a tidy, functional haven. This guide shows you why decluttering is crucial—it reduces stress, increases productivity, and enhances your living environment.

Learn how to approach decluttering systematically, from deciding what to keep, donate, or discard, to optimizing storage solutions like car and bike storage or utilizing self-storage. Whether you’re facing a renovation, downsizing, or just need a more organized space, this guide offers practical, easy-to-follow steps to help you achieve a clutter-free home.

1. Avoid Dumping Small Things on Tables

Dumping little items on a table is the most typical error made when storing them. Ensure that each item has a place to store it. To keep things in order, use hooks and other storage options.

Clear surfaces of clutter to keep order and cleanliness, and use baskets, shelves, and other storage options. When storing under the kitchen or bathroom sink, though, identify a place where your items will not tip over.

When planning to organise something in a different or new space, consider how you would like to see it organised. Which do you like better, a bookcase or a basket? A box or a drawer? Consider placing your many trinkets in a basket, for instance, if you have a lot of them. Remember to leave plenty of space underneath the table if you must put items there. If not, you might harm the surface.

2. Be Smart with Storage for Professional Home Organisers

An average person owns tons of different objects – from clothing to books to accessories. Some are functional, some sentimental, while others are just there! Imagine what our living space would look like if it were reduced to just the top twenty important items. If we had to remove a further ten items, what would those be? What if there were a method to extend the lifespan of some of those items, while also making long-term financial savings? “Make sure you pull everything off the category you are working on together from anywhere it lives in your house,” Kate Pawlowski, founding partner of Done & Done Home.

This is where it is important to be smart with how and what you store. Storage space, especially if you live in an expensive, urbane city, is premium. Be clear about what you are spending it on.

Also important is to look at how to store your items. Hooks are the best tool for keeping things organised. They are affordable, simple to use, and durable. Online, you can find many DIY (Do It Yourself) hook-making possibilities if you do not want to buy them. Open storage bins look wonderful and will aid in hiding clutter too. They are simple to keep and clean. Taking care of them will make them last indefinitely.

Professional organisers can help you be smart with your storage solutions by maximizing your space and minimizing clutter.

3. Keep things in boxes

Another method of organising things is with boxes. You have two options for boxes: you can buy them or construct your own from cardboard or plastic. In either case, ensure each box has enough space inside. Otherwise, it would be difficult for you to find exactly what you need.

  • Use box labels because it is simple to find what you are looking for if the items are labelled. Shoe boxes, drawers, and even the box itself can all be labelled.
  • Organise by type, for eg., you will be able to store your shoes, boots, sandals, and other footwear more effectively if you know how many pairs you own of each type.
  • Think about colour coding. It is simpler to see what belongs together when things are colour coded. Black shoes go in one bin, red shoes in another, etc.

 

Professional organisers can advise you on the best way to keep things in boxes, so you can maximize your storage space and stay organised.

4. Store Fewer Products

Here are some suggestions to help you in beautifying your home without spending a lot of money. It is not necessary to buy pricey furniture and accessories. Instead, consider the power of less:

  • Start by keeping fewer things around instead. You will have less clutter and will not feel like you are continually looking for items if you do it this way.
  • Making better decisions is what minimalism is all about, not necessarily cutting back on spending.
  • Do not place too much emphasis on quantity; instead, prioritise quality.
