1. Procedure For Renting Space & Agreement Overview

Welcome to Self Storage India (operated by Private Storage Solutions Pvt. Ltd.). By requesting space, signing a rental agreement, or occupying a storage room, locker, or warehouse bay at any of our facilities across Delhi NCR, you ("Lessee" or "Customer") agree to abide by these Terms and Conditions ("Terms").

Step-by-Step Space Allocation Procedure

  1. Space Selection & Quote Request: Customer submits space requirements or consults with our storage advisors to select an appropriate unit size (Locker, Private Room, Box Storage, or Vehicle Bay).
  2. Know Your Customer (KYC) Verification: Customer provides valid identity proof (Aadhaar Card, Passport, Voter ID) and address proof, alongside corporate incorporation documents for business accounts.
  3. Payment of Advance Rent & Security Deposit: Total Rent for the initial billing period (1st month's rent plus applicable Goods and Services Tax - GST) and the refundable Security Deposit (equal to 3 times the Total Rent) must be paid in full prior to unit allocation.
  4. Lease Agreement Dispatch & Signing: A standard, non-negotiable lease agreement is sent electronically via email or courier to the customer. The original physical agreement must be signed in person upon room takeover or item delivery.
  5. Key & Smart Access Provisioning: Lessee sets their personal lock or receives facility access credentials. Self Storage India employees do not retain master keys to lessee private locks under standard operations.
info Standard Lease Agreement Notice

All storage rentals are subject to our formal Lease Agreement. The terms outlined on this webpage reflect core operational procedures. In the event of any specific operational variance, the terms of the physically executed Lease Agreement will prevail.

2. Security Deposit Regulations & Refund Policy

To safeguard facility infrastructure, ensure compliance with lease commitments, and offset potential unpaid dues or repair costs, Self Storage India requires a refundable Security Deposit from every lessee prior to unit handover.

Security Deposit Terms & Conditions

  • Deposit Amount: The security deposit is fixed at an amount equivalent to three (3) times the Total Monthly Rent (Monthly Rent + GST).
  • Non-Interest Bearing: The security deposit is held by Self Storage India in a non-interest-bearing account for the entire duration of the lease.
  • Refund Eligibility Criteria:
    • Submission of a formal 30-day prior written notice via email to support@selfstorageindia.com.
    • The storage unit must be completely vacated, cleaned of all trash or debris, and left in the exact structural condition in which it was handed over.
    • All outstanding monthly rents, late fees, utility surcharges (if applicable), and service charges must be settled in full.
    • Removal of personal padlocks and return of any facility-provided access badges or keys.
  • Deductions & Forfeiture: Self Storage India reserves the explicit right to deduct funds from the security deposit to cover repair of structural damage caused by the lessee, removal of abandoned items, unpaid rent balances, or legal/administrative recovery fees.
  • Processing Timeline: Once the unit passes final move-out inspection and settlement of accounts, the net security deposit will be refunded via bank transfer (NEFT/RTGS/UPI) to the primary account holder within 7 to 10 business days.

3. Billing Cycle & Prorated Rent Calculations

Rent is billed on a calendar-month basis. All payments must be made on or before the due date specified in your invoice.

Prorated Rent Formula for Mid-Month Onboarding

When a lessee moves in during the middle or last week of a calendar month, rent for the initial billing cycle is calculated on a prorated basis to ensure fair billing:

Onboarding Date Initial Billing Requirement Prorated Formula
1st Day of Month 1 Month Total Rent + 3 Months Security Deposit Standard 1 Full Calendar Month
Middle of Month (e.g., 15th) Remaining Days of Current Month + 1 Full Subsequent Month + Security Deposit [(Total Rent / 30) × Remaining Days] + 1 Month Total Rent
Last Week of Month (e.g., 25th) Remaining Days of Current Month + 1 Full Subsequent Month + Security Deposit [(Total Rent / 30) × Remaining Days] + 1 Month Total Rent

Accepted Payment Methods

Self Storage India accepts payments via Online NetBanking (NEFT/RTGS/IMPS), UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), Credit Cards, Debit Cards, and recurring NACH/Auto-Debit mandates. Cash payments above statutory limit requirements are strictly prohibited.

5. Car & Vehicle Storage Regulations

Vehicles stored at our secure facility bays (Gurugram, Delhi, Noida) are subject to specialized security and regulatory checks:

  • Minimum Duration: Car storage requires a minimum rental period of seven (7) days.
  • Mandatory Documentation: Customer must provide a copy of the Vehicle Registration Certificate (RC), valid comprehensive insurance policy, and identity proof of the registered owner. Stored vehicles must be legally registered and free of police or court impoundment orders.
  • Key Deposit & Emergency Repositioning: A duplicate ignition key must be deposited with facility management to allow vehicle movement in the event of facility maintenance, fire drills, or emergency repositioning.
  • Vehicle Condition: Stored vehicles must be clean, free of fuel or oil leaks, and contain no hazardous, flammable, or illegal materials inside the boot or cabin. Batteries must be disconnected for storage periods exceeding 30 days.

6. Agreement Execution, Delivery & Nominee Designation

To streamline customer onboarding while maintaining strict legal enforcement, the following contract procedures apply:

  • Electronic & Courier Delivery: The standard lease contract is issued via email or courier upon booking confirmation.
  • In-Person Physical Signature: The original physical agreement must be signed in person by the lessee or an authorized signatory upon room allocation or inventory pickup.
  • Nominated Representative Policy: Lessees may designate up to two (2) nominated representatives authorized to access the unit on their behalf. Nominees must be officially registered in the lease agreement with submitted Aadhaar/ID proof. Access will be denied to unlisted third parties without prior written authorization from the primary lessee.

7. Facility Access Hours, Safety & Surveillance

Facility security is paramount. Self Storage India maintains high-tier multi-layered physical and digital security parameters across all locations:

  • Operating Access Hours: Customer access is available from 9:00 AM to 7:00 PM, Monday through Saturday. Sunday access or after-hours access must be scheduled 24 hours in advance with facility management.
  • Gate Authorization: Entry requires presentation of a registered access card, biometric verification, or official photo ID verification at the security desk. All visitors accompanying the lessee must log their details in the visitor register.
  • 24/7 CCTV Surveillance: Facility hallways, loading bays, perimeter gates, and common corridors are continuously monitored and recorded by high-definition CCTV cameras. Video footage is archived per statutory security compliance.
  • Fire Prevention & Pest Control: Facilities are outfitted with smoke detectors, fire extinguishers, and sprinkler systems, alongside regular professional pest control treatments.

8. Permitted Use & Strictly Prohibited Items

Storage units may strictly be used for the passive storage of approved personal household goods, office archives, furniture, commercial non-perishable inventory, and vehicles. Storing unauthorized items constitutes a material breach of contract and may lead to immediate termination, legal action, and notification of law enforcement.

warning Strictly Prohibited Goods & Substances

Under no circumstances may any lessee bring, store, or deposit the following items inside Self Storage India facilities:

  • Explosives & Munitions: Fireworks, gunpowder, ammunition, firearms, weapons, or military gear.
  • Flammables & Combustibles: Petrol, diesel, kerosene, lighter fluid, paints, thinners, solvents, or compressed gas cylinders.
  • Toxic & Hazardous Chemicals: Corrosive acids, radioactive materials, bio-hazardous waste, pesticides, or toxic chemicals.
  • Perishables & Foodstuff: Fresh food, raw produce, meat, agricultural goods prone to decay, or items attracting pests/vermin.
  • Living Organisms: Animals, birds, reptiles, insects, or live plants.
  • Illegal Contraband: Narcotics, illicit drugs, stolen property, smuggled goods, or items prohibited under Indian law.
  • High-Value Cash & Bullion: Unregistered currency notes, precious gemstones, gold/silver bullion, or negotiable financial securities (stored entirely at lessee risk unless explicitly declared and insured).

Self Storage India reserves the right to inspect any unit or package if there is reasonable suspicion of prohibited goods, chemical leaks, fire hazard, or upon formal order by law enforcement or judicial authorities.

9. Non-Payment, Late Fees & Statutory Lien Rights

Prompt payment of monthly rent is a prerequisite for ongoing unit access and storage services.

  • Due Date & Grace Period: Monthly rent is due on the 1st day of each calendar month. A grace period is extended until the 5th day of the month.
  • Late Payment Penalty: Payments received after the 5th day will incur a late fee of ₹500 or 2% per month (whichever is higher) on the outstanding balance.
  • Access Suspension: If rent remains unpaid for ten (10) consecutive days after the due date, customer biometric gate access and unit key access will be automatically suspended, and a secondary management lock placed on the unit.
  • Statutory Lien & Disposal Rights: Pursuant to Sections 170 and 171 of the Indian Contract Act, 1872, and industry guidelines, Self Storage India exercises a general possessory lien over all goods stored in the unit for unpaid rent, storage charges, late fees, and recovery expenses.

    If rent remains in default for more than forty-five (45) days despite formal written demand notices sent via registered post/email, Self Storage India shall have the absolute legal right to enter the unit, take possession of stored contents, and sell, auction, or dispose of the goods at public or private sale to recover outstanding dues and legal costs. Any excess proceeds after full debt settlement will be remitted to the lessee.

10. Insurance Options & Limitation of Liability

All items placed in storage are stored at the sole risk and responsibility of the Lessee.

  • Customer Insurance Responsibility: Lessee is required to maintain adequate insurance coverage for stored goods against perils including fire, flood, earthquake, burglary, theft, water leakage, and natural disasters.
  • Goods Protection Assistance: Self Storage India can assist lessees in purchasing third-party storage insurance policies upon request during onboarding.
  • Limitation of Company Liability: Self Storage India, its directors, officers, and employees shall not be liable for any indirect, consequential, or incidental damage, or for loss of goods resulting from natural disasters, acts of God, ambient humidity changes, pest activity beyond reasonable control, or unauthorized forced entry, except where such loss is directly proven to result from gross negligence or willful misconduct of Self Storage India. In all cases, the maximum aggregate liability of Self Storage India is strictly capped at an amount equal to one (1) month's rental fee paid by the lessee for the affected unit.

11. Privacy Policy, Data Handling & Nominees

Self Storage India respects customer privacy and strictly safeguards all personal data collected during the rental process:

  • Confidential Data Storage: Customer identification documents (Aadhaar, PAN, Passport, Corporate GSTIN) and contact details are stored securely for statutory compliance, identity verification, and billing purposes.
  • No Data Sharing: Self Storage India does not sell, rent, trade, or share customer personal information with third-party telemarketers or external entities.
  • Authorized Disclosures: Information may only be disclosed to government bodies, police, or judicial authorities when required under law, court summons, or regulatory compliance.

12. Lease Cancellation, Early Termination & Vacating

Lessees or Self Storage India may terminate the lease agreement subject to the following notice and move-out requirements:

  • Cancellation Prior to Move-In: Bookings cancelled seven (7) or more days prior to the agreed lease start date are eligible for a 100% refund of advance rent and security deposit. Bookings cancelled within 7 days of start date incur a 10% administrative cancellation fee.
  • 30-Day Move-Out Notice: Lessee must submit a written move-out notice via email at least 30 days prior to vacating. Rent will be charged for the full 30-day notice period regardless of early key return.
  • Final Inspection & Lock Removal: Upon move-out, lessee must remove all personal locks, empty the unit completely, and complete a joint exit inspection with the facility manager. Un-cleared units will continue to accrue daily storage charges.

13. Force Majeure & Emergency Events

Neither party shall be held liable for failure or delay in performing lease obligations if such performance is prevented or delayed by Force Majeure events, including but not limited to severe acts of God, floods, earthquakes, fires, lightning, pandemics, government lockdown orders, statutory property acquisitions, civil commotion, war, or acts of terrorism. During force majeure events, facility access hours may be restricted or suspended for safety reasons.

14. Governing Law, Jurisdiction & Dispute Resolution

These Terms and Conditions and all associated rental agreements shall be governed by, interpreted, and enforced in accordance with the laws of the Republic of India.

  • Amicable Resolution: In the event of any dispute, controversy, or claim arising out of or relating to storage services, the parties shall first attempt to resolve the issue amicably through good-faith negotiations.
  • Arbitration: If unresolved within thirty (30) days, the dispute shall be referred to arbitration in accordance with the Indian Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996. The arbitration shall be conducted by a sole arbitrator appointed by Self Storage India, and the venue of arbitration shall be New Delhi.
  • Exclusive Jurisdiction: The courts of New Delhi, India shall have exclusive jurisdiction over any legal proceedings arising from these terms.
